Fixes

  • Replaced the shared placeholder description on article pages with factual title-based descriptions when an original abstract or custom SEO description is unavailable.
  • Added the journal title to citation metadata and the Periodical hierarchy in article structured data. Existing author, publication-date and PDF citation tags remain available.
  • Gave page 2 and later archive/list pages their own canonical URL and page-specific title. Tracking parameters are excluded. Internal filtered listings use noindex, follow.
  • Added journal and resources pages to the sitemap. The preview sitemap now uses the preview host, matching its live canonical URLs. At launch both application and sitemap URLs must switch together to sjcjournal.com.
  • Changed generic homepage/archive descriptions to cardiology topics, added a relevant homepage title and heading, used the existing PNG logo for social-image defaults, and respected editorial Twitter card settings.
  • Connected saved editorial focus keywords to article schema. Safely encoded JSON-LD and enabled a Google Search Console verification tag when a real token is configured.

Validation: 166 application tests passed (1,552 assertions), followed by 12 focused SEO/settings tests after the default-setting adjustment. Production views compile successfully. A private backup of changed files and configuration was retained on Hostinger. The journal database, PDFs, author profiles and mail transport were preserved.

Keyword focus

These suggestions are inferred from the journal’s content and article titles. Search volume, competition, ranking and traffic were not measured. Assign one main topic to each page and use related phrases naturally in its title, description, abstract and headings. Google does not use the meta keywords tag for indexing or ranking; avoid adding a long keyword list to every page. See Google’s supported meta tags.

Work needed before launch

  1. Connect sjcjournal.com to the new Hostinger website, verify TLS for the chosen hostname, and keep a recoverable copy of the old site.
  2. Map old article/abstract/PDF URLs to their actual new equivalents and add permanent redirects. Preserve existing indexed URLs and backlinks; do not redirect every old article to the homepage.
  3. Set APP_URL and SITEMAP_BASE_URL to https://sjcjournal.com. Redirect alternate hostnames to the chosen canonical host. Check that the permanent domain’s robots.txt permits public content and advertises its own sitemap; keep private portals excluded.
  4. Restore original abstracts and author-supplied article keywords after checking the published PDFs. All 39 current articles lack editorial keywords in schema. Suggested phrases below are review material and were not written into scientific article metadata. Verify the registered ISSN/eISSN and DOI values before adding them.
  5. Verify domain ownership in Google Search Console, submit the permanent sitemap and inspect representative article URLs. Review Google Scholar inclusion requirements. Neither Google nor Scholar indexing has been confirmed by this audit.
  6. Measure mobile performance/Core Web Vitals and actual search queries after launch. No Lighthouse, field Core Web Vitals, Search Console or keyword-volume dataset was collected in this audit.
