## The SJC publication record
Singapore Journal of Cardiology brings together contributions addressing cardiovascular medicine and related clinical questions. The online collection includes original research, reviews and case-based discussion contributed by authors working across different institutions and settings.

## The renewed website
The current website brings article records, contributor profiles and full published PDFs into one reading experience. Imported articles retain their original publication dates and bibliographic details. The website migration itself is not a new research publication or a new peer-review event.

## Exploring earlier work
Use the [article collection](/articles) to browse by date or search for a title or contributor. Published PDFs preserve the original presentation, including author credits, figures and references. Where a record and PDF differ, contact the editorial office so the discrepancy can be checked.

## Historical enquiries
For verified details of founding dates, past editorial appointments, title changes or issue history, [contact the journal](/contact). Historical claims should be supported by publication records rather than inferred from the age of the current website.
