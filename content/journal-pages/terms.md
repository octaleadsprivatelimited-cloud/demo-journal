These terms describe responsible use of the Singapore Journal of Cardiology website, its accounts and submission facilities. Article-specific licences and any separately agreed publication or service terms continue to apply to the material they cover.

## Accounts and submissions
Provide accurate information and use only accounts and records you are authorised to access. Keep login credentials private and report suspected unauthorised use. Submit material only when you have the necessary rights, approvals and consent. A submission is a request for editorial consideration and does not guarantee acceptance, timing or publication.

## Permitted use
You may browse the public website and use material in accordance with its applicable rights statement. Do not upload malicious files, attempt unauthorised access, impersonate another person, misuse personal information or interfere with the service. Do not use contact or comment forms for spam or unrelated promotion.

## Publication and availability
The journal may moderate comments, correct records and restrict abusive use. The website and its content may change, and access may be interrupted for maintenance or other operational reasons. Scientific content is provided for scholarly information and is not individual medical advice; see the [Site Disclaimer](/policies/disclaimer).
