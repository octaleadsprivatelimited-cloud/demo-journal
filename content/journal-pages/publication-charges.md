## Obtain one clear account of the costs
The total cost of publication, if applicable, should be confirmed in writing. Ask whether the quotation includes any submission charge, article processing charge, additional preparation service or other charge, and whether taxes or transaction costs are additional.

## Optional services
A service offered separately should be described clearly, including whether it is optional. Payment for editing or another service must not be represented as a guarantee of acceptance or favourable review. Authors should not assume that a service provider can make an editorial decision.

## Payment and changes
Check the manuscript reference and authorised payment details before paying. Request clarification of discrepancies, duplicate invoices or a changed quotation. Confirm the cancellation and refund terms before committing; this page does not create an unverified refund promise or price schedule.
