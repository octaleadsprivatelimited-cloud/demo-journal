## Finding newly published work
The website lists published articles by publication date. Readers can browse [all articles](/articles) for recent additions or use [Current Issue / Archives](/current-issue) to find material organised into publication records where those records are available.

## Article dates
An article’s publication date identifies when that work entered the publication record. A later website update or migration does not establish a new original publication date. When a substantive correction is made, readers should consult the associated notice.

## Schedules and decisions
Review, revision and preparation depend on the work required for an individual manuscript. Submission, an invitation or an editorial discussion does not guarantee publication in a particular issue or by a particular date. Authors with a timing constraint should discuss it with the editorial office before submission.

## Schedule enquiries
Ask the [editorial office](/contact) for the current publication schedule and issue arrangements. A fixed number of issues or a guaranteed turnaround should be relied on only when the journal has expressly confirmed it.
