## Reading published work
Published SJC articles made available on this website can be read without a reader subscription. Where a PDF is available, the article page provides an on-site reader and a download link. Unpublished submissions and confidential review materials are restricted to authorised participants.

## Access and reuse
Open access to read an article does not mean that every use is unrestricted. Check the licence printed on the individual article, including any exclusions for third-party material. Keep author attribution and source information when sharing work as permitted by that licence.

## Publication costs
Reader access and author publication charges are separate matters. Consult the [APC page](/policies/fees) and obtain written confirmation from the editorial office of any applicable charge before making a financial commitment.

## Availability concerns
Report a missing file, broken link or access problem through the [contact page](/contact), including the article title and the link that failed. An access interruption does not change the copyright or licence attached to the publication.
