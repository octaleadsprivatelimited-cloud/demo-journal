## How editorial responsibilities are organised
The journal’s editorial work brings together manuscript administration, subject expertise and independent assessment. Current named appointments are listed on the [Editorial Board](/editorial-board); a contributor profile does not itself indicate an editorial appointment.

## Editorial leadership
Editors-in-chief oversee editorial standards, address escalated concerns and guide final publication decisions. A handling editor assesses a manuscript’s relevance, selects appropriate reviewers, evaluates their reports and communicates the decision to the corresponding author.

## Editorial office and reviewers
The editorial office assists with submission completeness, correspondence, files and publication preparation. Reviewers provide specialist advice on the work’s methods, interpretation and reporting. Their recommendations inform the decision; they are not a vote that automatically determines the outcome.

## Accountability
Editors must disclose interests that could affect impartial handling and transfer conflicted manuscripts to another editor. Questions about a manuscript should include its reference number and be sent through the author workspace or [contact form](/contact). Complaints about editorial conduct follow the [complaints and appeals process](/policies/complaints-appeals).
