## Information used by the journal
The website processes information you provide when creating an account, submitting a manuscript, completing a review, sending an enquiry, posting a comment or subscribing to updates. This may include your name, email, affiliation, account details, uploaded files and correspondence. Technical records such as network address, request time and session information support website operation and security.

## Purposes and visibility
Information is used to administer accounts, assess and publish submissions, handle correspondence and operate the journal. Author names, affiliations and published work may form part of the public scholarly record. Approved guest comments display the supplied name; the comment email is not displayed with the comment. Do not submit patient identifiers or confidential records through public-facing fields.

## Access and service providers
Unpublished manuscripts and reviews are intended for authorised editorial participants. Hosting and other service providers may process information needed to deliver the website. The production website is hosted on Microsoft Azure in India; access from other countries may involve cross-border processing. An external link has its own privacy arrangements.

## Cookies and retention
Session and request-security cookies support sign-in and forms. You may control cookies in your browser, but disabling essential cookies can prevent these functions. Editorial records, correspondence and security logs may need to be retained for operational, integrity or applicable legal purposes; deleting an account does not automatically remove a published scholarly record.

## Enquiries and requests
Use the [privacy contact channel](/contact?category=privacy) to ask about information held about you, request correction or raise a privacy concern. Explain the request without sending a password or unnecessary identification documents. The office may need to verify your identity and explain any limits affecting the request. Newsletter recipients may use the unsubscribe option in a message or contact the journal.
