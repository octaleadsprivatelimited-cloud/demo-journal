## Request support early
Authors who cannot meet a quoted publication charge may ask the editorial office about available support. Submit the request before agreeing to payment, preferably when first discussing the submission. A request does not guarantee a full or partial waiver.

## Information to provide
Include the manuscript reference or proposed title, article type, institution and a brief explanation of the funding available for publication. State whether an institutional fund or research grant can contribute. Supply supporting information only when requested and avoid sending unnecessary personal financial records.

## Written confirmation
Any waiver or discount should be confirmed in writing, with the amount, scope and conditions clearly stated. Do not assume that a previous award, an invitation to submit or an informal conversation changes an invoice.

## Editorial independence
Financial assistance must not influence the scientific assessment of a manuscript or guarantee acceptance. Questions about available arrangements should go through the [fees contact channel](/contact?category=fees), not to a peer reviewer.
