## Publishing and editorial enquiries
Singapore Journal of Cardiology is presented under the Larix International publishing identity shown on this website. The journal’s editorial and publisher contact channels are available on the [Contact Us page](/contact).

## Different responsibilities
The editorial team manages scientific assessment, reviewer selection and manuscript decisions. Publishing operations support the website, article preparation, records and author correspondence. A service provider or sponsor should not be treated as an authority to accept a manuscript.

## Confirm official information
For a contract, invoice, institutional record or rights request, ask the editorial office to confirm the responsible legal entity and current business details in writing. A journal title, website brand and registered legal entity are not necessarily identical.

## Genuine correspondence
Use the contact information displayed on the journal website to verify unexpected requests. Include the manuscript reference when asking about a submission or invoice. Do not share account passwords or make payments on the basis of an unverified message claiming to represent the publisher.
