## Who should be an author?
Authorship recognises intellectual work and carries responsibility. A named author should have made a substantial contribution to the work, participated in drafting or critical revision, approved the version to be published and accepted accountability for its integrity. Funding, supervision or administrative assistance alone is not sufficient.

## Contribution statements
Describe each author’s contribution in plain language, such as study design, data collection, analysis or manuscript revision. Acknowledge other assistance with permission. Automated tools cannot take responsibility for a publication and must not be listed as authors.

## Corresponding author
The corresponding author coordinates submission, ensures that co-authors approve the manuscript and declarations, and responds to editorial queries. Names, affiliations and contact information must be checked before publication.

## Changes and disputes
Explain any proposed addition, removal or reordering of authors and obtain written agreement from all affected authors. Editors may pause handling when an authorship dispute is unresolved. Institutions may need to determine contributions where the journal cannot verify them.
