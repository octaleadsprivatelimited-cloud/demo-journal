## Prepare a complete submission
Choose a topic within the [Aims & Scope](/policies/aims-scope) and identify the article type. Prepare a clear title, author names and affiliations, corresponding-author details, an abstract where appropriate, keywords and the manuscript files.

## Required declarations
Confirm that the work is original, is not simultaneously under consideration elsewhere, has the approval of all named authors and satisfies applicable ethics requirements. Disclose competing interests and funding. State publication consent for identifiable material, data availability and any substantive use of AI tools. A declaration must be accurate; ticking a box does not replace supporting documentation.

## Files and references
Use legible figures, editable tables where appropriate and complete reference details. Include permissions and supplementary files where needed. Image uploads must fit the website’s 5 MB limit per image. Follow the limits shown for each other upload field.

## Submission and revision
Submit through the [author workspace](/author/dashboard). Incomplete declarations can prevent submission. Track editorial requests in the workspace and answer revisions point by point. Before publication, check proofs carefully for author details, numerical values, references and figures. See [Manuscript Preparation](/policies/manuscript-preparation).
