## Explain where the evidence can be found
Include a data availability statement describing the data supporting the findings, where they can be accessed and any conditions or restrictions. A statement should be specific enough for a reader to understand what is available; avoid a general promise that cannot be fulfilled.

## Data, code and methods
Where appropriate, deposit data and analysis code in a suitable repository and cite the persistent record. State software versions, relevant parameters and material departures from the planned analysis. Distinguish the data underlying the article from supplementary illustrations or example files.

## Protect confidentiality
Open sharing must respect consent, privacy, intellectual property and other applicable restrictions. Do not upload patient-level identifiers. For restricted data, explain the reason, the type of access available, the responsible contact and any approval required. Controlled access may be more appropriate than unrestricted publication.

## Author responsibility
Authors should retain the records needed to support their findings and cooperate with reasonable editorial enquiries. Corrections to a dataset or code repository should be versioned and explained, particularly when they affect the article’s conclusions.
