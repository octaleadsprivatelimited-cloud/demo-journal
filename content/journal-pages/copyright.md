## Rights in submitted work
Authors are responsible for submitting material they have the right to publish. Submission does not, by itself, transfer copyright or establish a reuse licence. The applicable publication agreement and the rights statement on each article determine the rights granted for publication and reuse.

## Third-party material
Check the permission or licence for every borrowed figure, table, photograph, questionnaire and substantial extract. A citation acknowledges a source but does not necessarily authorise reproduction. Supply permission documentation when required and include the requested credit line.

## Reusing journal content
Consult the article’s copyright and licence statement before reproducing or adapting it. Material attributed to a third party may have different conditions from the surrounding article. If the rights statement is absent or unclear, contact the editorial office with the article details and proposed use.

## Copyright concerns
Use the [contact form](/contact) to identify the material concerned, explain your rights and provide supporting information. The journal may request clarification and consider correction, restricted access or other appropriate action after reviewing the evidence.
