## 1. Set up your author profile
Use the author registration and sign-in pages. Enter accurate contact details and keep your password private. Maintain the name and affiliation you want the editorial office to use in correspondence.

## 2. Prepare the record
Create a manuscript in the author workspace. Add its title, summary, article details and author information. Upload the required manuscript and any relevant supplementary material. Use filenames that make each document’s purpose clear.

## 3. Complete the declarations
Read every declaration and confirm only statements that are true. Missing required confirmations prevent submission. Resolve originality, concurrent-submission, author-approval, ethics or competing-interest concerns before proceeding; a draft can be retained while information is gathered.

## 4. Submit and follow progress
Check the record and files, then submit for editorial consideration. Use the workspace to follow the status and respond to requests. For a revision, upload the updated manuscript and a response explaining how each issue was addressed. Avoid creating duplicate submissions to chase a decision.

For help, [contact the journal](/contact) with the manuscript reference and a description of the problem.
