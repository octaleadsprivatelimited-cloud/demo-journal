## Website and article material
Rights in an individual article are governed by the copyright and licence statements attached to that article. Rights in website text, branding, design and other assets may be held separately by the publisher or the relevant rights holder.

## Do not assume one rule covers every item
A website footer notice does not replace an article’s open licence. Equally, an article’s reuse licence may not cover publisher logos, website design or third-party illustrations. Check the credit line and licence for the particular material you want to use.

## Permission enquiries
Where permission is needed or the rights statement is unclear, provide the item’s title or link, the portion you want to use, your intended audience and the proposed format. Contact the editorial office before distributing an adaptation whose terms are uncertain.
