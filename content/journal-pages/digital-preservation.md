## Access to the journal record
The website maintains published article records and associated files so readers can locate and consult them. Article identifiers, publication dates and author details help connect a record with its published manuscript.

## Backups and preservation are different
Operational backups support recovery from accidental deletion or service failure. They are not the same as participation in an independent, long-term scholarly preservation programme. This page does not claim membership in a named preservation service without a verified participation record.

## Changes and continuity
When a record changes, substantive corrections should be identified through a linked notice. Readers should retain the article’s bibliographic details and use persistent identifiers where available. Authors should also retain their research and publication records in accordance with their institutional requirements.

## Reporting a problem
If an article or supplementary file is unavailable, [send its title and link to the journal](/contact). Requests for information about long-term preservation arrangements should be directed to the publisher through the same contact channel.
