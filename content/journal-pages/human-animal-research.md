## Research involving people
Report the ethics committee’s approval or exemption and the consent arrangements. Explain participant selection, privacy safeguards and any measures relevant to vulnerable groups. Describe the applicable ethical framework and local requirements rather than relying on a general statement that the study was ethical.

## Clinical trials
Identify the trial registry and registration number, include the registration date and explain any registration after recruitment began. Provide a protocol or link where available, disclose changes to outcomes and describe data-sharing arrangements. Prospective registration is the approach recommended by [ICMJE](https://www.icmje.org/recommendations/browse/publishing-and-editorial-issues/clinical-trial-registration.html).

## Animal studies
Identify the institutional oversight body and authorisation. Describe species, sample-size reasoning, housing, procedures, pain management and humane endpoints as relevant. Explain how the design considered replacement, reduction and refinement of animal use.

## Transparent reporting
Use reporting guidance suited to the study design and provide enough methodological detail for readers to understand the work. Editors may request supporting documentation or clarification before review or publication.
