## Transparent support
Sponsorship should be identified so readers can distinguish financial or material support from independent editorial judgment. A sponsored activity, collection or publication should explain the sponsor’s identity and the nature of the support where relevant.

## No purchase of an editorial outcome
A sponsor may not require a favourable peer-review outcome, select a compliant reviewer, suppress results or dictate an author’s conclusions. Manuscripts associated with supported activities remain subject to appropriate editorial assessment and ethical requirements.

## Author and editor disclosures
Authors must report relevant funding and describe the funder’s involvement in study design, analysis, writing or submission. Editors and reviewers must disclose relevant relationships and arrange independent handling when necessary. Sponsorship should not be hidden in a general acknowledgement when a clearer explanation is needed.

## Proposals and concerns
Discuss a proposed arrangement with the [editorial office](/contact) before making commitments or using the journal’s identity. Sponsorship opportunities and terms require express agreement; this page is not an offer. Concerns about undeclared support may be raised through [Competing Interests](/policies/competing-interests) or the complaints channel.
