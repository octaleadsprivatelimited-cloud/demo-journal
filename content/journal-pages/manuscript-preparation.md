## Title page and summary
Provide a specific title, accurate author names and affiliations, corresponding-author contact details and relevant declarations. Include an abstract and keywords appropriate to the article type. Avoid abbreviations in the title unless they are widely understood.

## Main text
Present the question, methods, findings and interpretation in a logical order. Explain participant selection, measures, analysis and limitations. Give units, define abbreviations at first use and distinguish prespecified analysis from later exploration. Do not present a statistical association as proof of causation.

## Tables, figures and files
Number tables and figures in the order cited, supply self-contained legends and check that labels remain readable. Upload research images without misleading alterations. Keep each image within the 5 MB upload limit and retain the original source files. Identify supplementary files clearly and refer to them in the text.

## Final checks
Remove patient identifiers and unnecessary personal information. Check citations against original sources, document permissions and include the required declarations. Follow any anonymisation instructions supplied for the manuscript’s review; do not assume the website removes identifying information automatically.
