## Continue the scientific conversation
Readers can use the comment form on articles where discussion is enabled. Comments should address the article’s evidence, methods, interpretation or implications and give enough context for other readers to understand the point.

## Before you submit
Identify relevant competing interests. Refer to the passage, figure or finding you are discussing and include supporting references where useful. Do not include patient identifiers, private correspondence, defamatory allegations, advertising or unrelated personal information.

## Moderation
Comments are submitted for review and do not appear immediately. Moderation considers relevance, civility and substance. The name supplied with a guest comment may be shown publicly after approval; the email field is not displayed with the comment. Authors may respond through the same moderated process.

## Substantive concerns
A comment does not itself amend the publication record. If you believe findings are unreliable or an ethical issue requires investigation, use the [research-concern channel](/contact?category=ethics). Corrections and retractions follow the separate [publication-notice policy](/policies/corrections-retractions).
