## Keeping the publication record accurate
Readers and authors should report significant errors promptly, identifying the article and explaining how the issue affects its results, interpretation or attribution. The editorial office assesses the evidence and seeks an author response where appropriate.

## Types of notice
A correction addresses an error that can be resolved without invalidating the central findings. An expression of concern alerts readers to a substantial unresolved issue. A retraction identifies work whose reliability or ethical basis is seriously compromised. The appropriate response depends on the evidence, not simply on who first reported the problem.

## Transparency
A publication notice should identify the affected article, explain the reason for the action and remain connected to its record. Corrections must not quietly replace substantive findings without an explanation. Legal or privacy restrictions may limit the detail that can be made public.

## Reporting and finding notices
Send concerns through the [ethics contact channel](/contact?category=ethics). Published notices can be found in the [Corrections / Retractions Archive](/corrections-retractions). General reference: [COPE retraction guidance](https://doi.org/10.24318/cope.2019.1.4).
