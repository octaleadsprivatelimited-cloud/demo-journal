## Distinguish advertising from scholarship
Any advertising accepted for display must be recognisable as advertising and clearly separated from editorial content. An advertisement does not confer endorsement by the journal or influence the assessment of a manuscript.

## Content expectations
Advertising must identify the responsible advertiser and must not make misleading or unsupported claims. Material that promotes unlawful activity, impersonates the journal, misuses personal information or disguises a commercial message as independent research is unsuitable. Health-related claims require particular care and must meet applicable requirements.

## Editorial independence
Advertisers may not choose reviewers, influence acceptance decisions or suppress critical scholarly discussion. Commercial placement must not obstruct readers’ access to the article or its notices.

## Enquiries and complaints
Contact the [editorial office](/contact) about the availability and terms of advertising; this page does not state that placements are currently offered. To report an advertisement, include its location, advertiser and the reason for concern. The journal may review, reject or remove material that does not meet its requirements.
