## Scholarly information
The journal publishes research, analysis and discussion for educational and scholarly use. An article is not personalised medical advice and cannot account for an individual patient’s circumstances. Readers should consult an appropriately qualified professional for clinical decisions.

## Assess the evidence
Interpret findings in the context of the study design, limitations and wider evidence. Publication does not make every conclusion definitive. Clinical practice and scientific understanding can change, and readers should check for corrections, retractions and more recent information before relying on an article.

## Opinions and external material
Authors are responsible for their stated views. Their opinions do not automatically represent the journal, publisher, editors or affiliated institutions. Mention of a product, service or organisation does not by itself constitute endorsement. Links to external resources are provided for reference and are subject to the external site’s own content and terms.

## Reporting problems
Report material inaccuracies or unsafe, misleading or otherwise concerning content through [Contact Us](/contact). Substantive concerns about reliability are handled through the journal’s [publication-notice process](/policies/corrections-retractions).
