## Approval or exemption must be explained
Research involving people, human material, identifiable information or animals must have the oversight appropriate to its design and setting. State the responsible review body, decision date and approval or reference number. If formal review was not required, explain the basis for exemption and who determined it.

## Protect participants
Describe measures taken to protect participants’ welfare, dignity and privacy. Additional safeguards may be necessary for people whose circumstances limit their ability to make a free decision. Consent to take part in research and consent to publish identifiable material are separate questions.

## Documentation
Editors may ask for approval letters, protocol information or an explanation of amendments. Do not upload identifiable consent forms or private participant records as public supplementary material. Contact the editorial office to arrange appropriate handling of confidential evidence.

## When concerns arise
An unexplained absence of required approval may prevent further consideration. Retrospective editorial permission cannot replace research oversight. See [Informed Consent](/policies/informed-consent) and [Human & Animal Research Ethics](/policies/human-animal-research).
