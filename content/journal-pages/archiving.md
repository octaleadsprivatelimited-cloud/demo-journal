## Author self-archiving
Authors wishing to place a manuscript in an institutional or subject repository should check the publication agreement and the licence attached to the relevant version. A submitted manuscript, an accepted manuscript and the final published PDF may have different reuse conditions.

## Identify the version
Label the deposited file accurately and include the article title, authors, journal name and publication details. Where available, link to the DOI or article record so readers can find the version of record and any subsequent notices. Do not describe an unreviewed draft as an accepted or published article.

## Restrictions and updates
Confirm any applicable embargo or third-party restriction before depositing a file. When an article is corrected or retracted, update the repository record or add a link to the notice rather than leaving readers unaware of the change.

## Clarification
If the agreement or licence does not clearly cover your intended deposit, [contact the editorial office](/contact). Website access and operational backups are addressed separately in [Digital Preservation](/policies/digital-preservation).
