## Consider the invitation
Accept a review only when you have the expertise, time and independence to assess the manuscript. Declare relevant interests before accepting. If the deadline is unsuitable, contact the editor promptly rather than leaving the invitation unanswered.

## Read for substance
Assess the question, study design, ethics, methods, results and interpretation. Explain whether the evidence supports the conclusions and identify limitations that materially affect reliability. Check whether figures, tables and references allow the work to be understood. Do not require authors to make the paper resemble your own preferred study when its actual design is appropriate.

## Write a useful report
Separate major concerns from minor presentation points. Give specific, constructive reasons and distinguish essential changes from suggestions. Keep the recommendation consistent with the report. Put confidential concerns in the editor-only field, but do not hide scientific criticism there that authors need in order to respond.

## Submit through the reviewer workspace
Use the assigned record in the [reviewer dashboard](/reviewer/dashboard). Do not contact authors directly, share the manuscript or delegate the review without the editor’s permission. See [Reviewer Code of Conduct](/policies/reviewer-conduct).
