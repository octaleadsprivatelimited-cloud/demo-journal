## Decisions based on the manuscript
Editorial consideration should reflect relevance, methodological soundness, ethical conduct and clear reporting. Author status, commercial relationships or ability to pay must not determine acceptance. Review recommendations assist the editor but do not replace editorial judgment.

## Consistent handling
The journal may return incomplete submissions, decline unsuitable work before review, request revision or seek additional assessment. Acceptance remains subject to resolving material ethical and publication requirements. Authors should be told the decision and the significant reasons or changes required.

## Conflicted submissions
Editors must disclose relevant interests and arrange independent handling when they are involved in the research or have another material conflict. An editor’s own work should not be assessed solely by that editor. Invited material and special collections remain subject to suitable editorial scrutiny and disclosure.

## Record and accountability
Keep the manuscript history, decisions and material correspondence in the editorial record. Explain substantive post-publication changes through a notice. Authors and readers may raise concerns using [Complaints & Appeals](/policies/complaints-appeals). Advertising or sponsorship must be separated from manuscript decisions.
